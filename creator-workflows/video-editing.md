# Video Editing Workflow

## Assembly

Organize media and build the story in sequence.

## Structure

Remove unnecessary material and establish pacing.

## Continuity

Check screen direction, eyelines, movement, timing, and visual consistency.

## Rhythm

Use shot duration, movement, sound, and transitions to control pace.

## Finishing

Perform color, audio, graphics, captions, quality control, and delivery checks after the edit is structurally stable.
