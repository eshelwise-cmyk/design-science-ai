# Color Spaces

A color space defines how color information is represented within a specified model or encoding system.

## Workflow considerations

Color management should account for:

- capture color space
- working color space
- display or delivery space
- transfer characteristics
- gamut
- bit depth

A grading decision should be made with the intended color-management pipeline in mind. Do not treat a display transform, LUT, and creative grade as interchangeable operations.
