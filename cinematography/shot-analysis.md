# Shot Analysis

Use this sequence when analyzing a cinematic frame or shot.

1. Subject and narrative function
2. Shot size
3. Camera height and angle
4. Lens and perspective cues
5. Framing and negative space
6. Foreground, middle ground, background
7. Lighting direction and contrast
8. Color palette and tonal structure
9. Camera movement if applicable
10. Relationship between visual choices and viewer attention

Separate observations from interpretation. Do not infer camera settings that cannot reasonably be determined from the image.
