# Framing Techniques

Framing directs attention by using elements inside or around the image to define a subject.

## Types

- Natural framing
- Architectural framing
- Geometric framing
- Light and shadow framing
- Foreground framing
- Layered framing
- Implied framing

## Analysis

Identify the frame, the subject it isolates, the depth relationship it creates, and whether it adds context or simply blocks distractions.
