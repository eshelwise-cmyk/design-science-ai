# Cinematography Visual Language

Cinematography uses framing, camera position, movement, lens choice, lighting, color, depth, and timing to communicate information and create atmosphere.

## Scene analysis

1. Identify the subject and narrative purpose.
2. Examine framing and shot size.
3. Examine camera position and movement.
4. Examine light and contrast.
5. Examine color relationships.
6. Identify how these choices direct attention.

A cinematic image is not defined by a single aspect ratio or color palette. Intentional visual language and storytelling choices matter more than a preset look.
