# Human Vision and Color Perception

Color perception depends on illumination, surrounding colors, adaptation, display conditions, and the visual system.

## Grading implications

- White balance is judged relative to the viewing context.
- Contrast changes perceived color relationships.
- Surrounding colors can alter perceived neutrality.
- A grade should be evaluated under controlled viewing conditions when accuracy matters.

Color psychology can inform creative intent, but claims about universal emotional responses should be treated cautiously and supported by evidence.
